# Using Workflow Templates for Users Without a Blink Account

> Instructions on how to use a Workflow template if the user has not signed up for the Blink Platform

While browsing our online [Blink Library](https://library.blinkops.com/workflows), and you find a Workflow that you want to try, use it as follows:

1. In the Blink Library webpage, click the name of the Workflow you are interested in. The Workflow details will be displayed. Click the "Get Started" button to be directed to the [Blink Sign Up Page](https://www.blinkops.com/schedule-time)

   <Frame>
     <img src="https://mintcdn.com/blinkops-2/0JKaSiVOyIaH-NI8/img/GettingStarted/NonUserLibraryAccess.gif?s=a4839fbf507dec062b4525db0984b6c9" width="1800" height="1080" data-path="img/GettingStarted/NonUserLibraryAccess.gif" />
   </Frame>

**OR**

2. On the Workflow Library page, click the "Get Started" button. You are taken to the [Blink Sign Up Page](https://www.blinkops.com/schedule-time).

   <Frame>
     <img src="https://mintcdn.com/blinkops-2/0JKaSiVOyIaH-NI8/img/GettingStarted/SignUpAccessLibrary.gif?s=b5b74bed7f1fc36bc9fab44bf50a4cc3" width="1800" height="1080" data-path="img/GettingStarted/SignUpAccessLibrary.gif" />
   </Frame>

   1. Complete the sign-up process, and you will receive a confirmation email once you have approved.
   2. Click Log in to your account to be directed to the Workflow page in your workspace.
   3. Click on the *"Hello World"* Workflow sample. You will be redirected to the Workflow Editor.
   4. Use the *"Hello World"* sample template to practice editing a Workflow.
   5. Once you are finished, publish the *"Hello World"* sample. The template you selected earlier will now appear on your Workflow page in your workspace, automatically placed in your Home pack.
