# About Workflow Packs

> Organize and manage workflows using packs, which group related workflows together in Blink.

A Pack is a collection of automated workflows that share a common purpose, goal, or operational domain. In Blink, every workflow must belong to a Pack. This built-in structure keeps workflows logically organized and easier to manage over time, especially in large workspaces with many automated workflows.

<Tip>We recommend grouping your Packs by topic, business function, functional area, or related vendors to keep everything organized and easy to manage, for example: **AWS Cloud Cost Optimization** </Tip>
