# Sharing a Connection

> Share connections with other users or workspaces for collaborative workflow development in Blink.

You can share a connection you created with other workspaces, allowing their members to use it in their workflows. This is useful for enabling collaboration across teams or standardizing access to external services.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Share Settings for a Connection">
    <p>  Navigate to the **Connections** page. Click the <Icon icon="square-ellipsis-vertical" iconType="solid" /> icon next to the connection you want to share, then select **Share**. </p>

    <Frame>
      <img src="https://mintcdn.com/blinkops-2/LTL4io7BmEAPsFrF/img/Connections/ShareConnection.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=LTL4io7BmEAPsFrF&q=85&s=efb73e638d465a7ce05975f9b408af10" width="1727" height="1083" data-path="img/Connections/ShareConnection.png" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select Workspaces to Share With">
    Use the search bar to find and select the workspaces you want to share the connection with.

    <Frame>
      <img src="https://mintcdn.com/blinkops-2/LTL4io7BmEAPsFrF/img/Connections/ChooseWorkspace.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=LTL4io7BmEAPsFrF&q=85&s=f2d3d3099b8e45321ce6383d085e55ba" width="3456" height="2160" data-path="img/Connections/ChooseWorkspace.png" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Confirm the Share">
    Click **Add** to share the connection. Members of the selected workspace(s) can now use the connection in their workflows.

    <Frame>
      <img src="https://mintcdn.com/blinkops-2/LTL4io7BmEAPsFrF/img/Connections/ConnectionWorkspace.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=LTL4io7BmEAPsFrF&q=85&s=881b0a875b982e108f3adcaf00c802d1" width="3446" height="2150" data-path="img/Connections/ConnectionWorkspace.png" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review Sharing Details">
    The information box shows all workspaces the connection is shared with, along with the email address of the user who shared it.

    <Frame>
      <img src="https://mintcdn.com/blinkops-2/LTL4io7BmEAPsFrF/img/Connections/SharedWithField.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=LTL4io7BmEAPsFrF&q=85&s=4e61524b83df9933e5e43ee3cd73c3f9" width="1726" height="1077" data-path="img/Connections/SharedWithField.png" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>
